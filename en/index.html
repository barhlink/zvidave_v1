Vrstvička – an inquiry workshop on 3D printing

  • Computing
  • Years 3–6
  • about 15 minutes
  • in Czech

Children find out how a 3D printer is set up. They choose an object, pick the print settings and watch the result. When a print fails, they adjust the settings and try again. Step by step, they discover what each setting does.

How to use it in a lesson

  1. First, children predict what the print will look like.
  2. They check their prediction in the game and record the result in their research journal.
  3. At the end of the lesson, discuss as a class which predictions were right.

About

Who makes the games

I teach computing and maths at a Czech school and also work as a graphic designer. I make the games on Zvídavě because I couldn’t find the kind I needed for my own teaching.

– Baru

Principles behind the games

  1. 1

    No leaderboards

    The games don’t time children or keep score, and children don’t compete against each other. Every child works at their own pace.

  2. 2

    Mistakes are fine

    Instead of a red cross, children see what happened and can try again.

  3. 3

    Calm design

    A correct answer doesn’t set off confetti or flashing animations, so children can focus on the task itself.
